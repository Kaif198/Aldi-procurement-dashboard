Strategic Procurement Insights

🎯 Supplier Consolidation Impact

Reduced supplier base by 3.4% YoY while maintaining quality standards. This consolidation resulted in €42M additional cost savings through volume leverage and streamlined operations.

♻️ Sustainability Progress

89% of suppliers now meet ALDI's sustainability standards, up from 78% last year. All key suppliers committed to eliminating deforestation by end of 2025, aligning with corporate goals.

🚀 Digital Transformation

Implemented One Network Intelligent Control Tower for global shipping visibility. Real-time collaboration with 1,847 suppliers has reduced procurement cycle time by 15% quarter-over-quarter.

📊 Spend Under Management

Achieved 72.4% spend under contract, approaching world-class benchmark of 75%. Focus areas: Non-food items and regional logistics services for further contract coverage improvement.

🌍 Regional Optimization

European suppliers account for 68% of total spend, with increasing focus on local sourcing to reduce transportation costs and carbon footprint. Asian sourcing optimized for non-food categories.

⚡ Efficiency Gains

JIT inventory system integration with top suppliers reduced warehousing costs by €18M annually. EDI automation achieved 97% invoice accuracy, significantly reducing manual interventions.
